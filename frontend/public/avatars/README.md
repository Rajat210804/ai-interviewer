# Interviewer portraits

These are the six Unsplash stock portraits already used by the application. They are bundled locally so the setup page and interview room can display the same avatars without a third-party image request.

The photos are used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). The [Unsplash terms](https://unsplash.com/terms) describe the service and its image licensing. Source URLs are retained below, and `provenance.json` records each downloaded file's dimensions, size and SHA-256 digest.

The names in the application describe fictional AI personas. They do not identify the people pictured, and the photographs do not imply participation in or endorsement of this application.
